# Incident Handler's Journal – Ransomware Incident

## Overview

This project documents my first incident handler's journal entry as part of my cybersecurity training.

The scenario involves a small U.S. healthcare clinic that experienced a ransomware attack after employees received targeted phishing emails containing a malicious attachment.

## Incident Summary

* **Incident Type:** Ransomware attack
* **Initial Attack Vector:** Phishing email
* **Malicious Content:** Email attachment
* **Impact:** Critical files, including medical records, were encrypted
* **Business Impact:** Healthcare operations were disrupted
* **Threat Actor:** Organized cybercriminal group
* **Ransom Demand:** Payment in exchange for a decryption key

## The 5 W's

### Who?

An organized group of unethical hackers targeting healthcare and transportation organizations.

### What?

Attackers used targeted phishing emails containing a malicious attachment to gain access and deploy ransomware.

### When?

The incident occurred on Tuesday at approximately 9:00 a.m.

### Where?

The incident affected the computer network and systems of a small U.S. primary-care healthcare clinic.

### Why?

The attackers sought to gain unauthorized access, encrypt critical files, and demand payment for a decryption key.
